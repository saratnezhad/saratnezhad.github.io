How I work

The same three steps on every project

01
Observe first

Field observation and interviews before any screen. What people say and what they do are rarely the same.

02
Prototype the version worth testing

Flows and interactive Figma prototypes on a design system, so what gets tested is close to what ships.

03
Measure, then polish

Task time, error rates, gaze metrics. If the numbers say the design did not help, the design changes.
